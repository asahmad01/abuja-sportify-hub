For fans · How it works

Home screen to front row.

Events live right on the Spotts home screen, next to the courts you already book. Your ticket is a QR code — it can't get lost, and it scans in a second.

01

Spot it on the home screen

Tournaments, finals and featured game nights appear alongside venues — no separate app, no ticket site.

02

Pay in seconds

The same secure checkout you use for court bookings. Pay, and the ticket is yours — no confirmation emails to dig for.

03

QR ticket, instantly

A unique QR code lands in your app the moment payment clears. It's your ticket — nobody else can use it.

04

Scan at the gate

Staff scan your code and you're in — checked in against the guest list in real time. No printouts, no name-checking.
