Common topics, answered fast.

Cancelling a booking

Open your booking in the app and tap Cancel Booking. You'll see the exact refund amount — based on the venue's policy you accepted at booking — before you confirm. Refunds process automatically.

Where's my refund?

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method in 3–10 working days, depending on your bank. Past 10 days, check with your bank first, then email us. Full details in the Refund Policy.

The venue cancelled on me

You get a full 100% refund, including processing fees — automatically, no matter how close to your slot the cancellation happened.

Payment issues

Payments run through Paystack's secure checkout — Spotts never stores your card number or CVV. If a payment was charged but a booking didn't confirm, email us with the transaction reference and we'll trace it.

Reporting a review or message

Use the in-app report button on any review or open-session message that breaks the rules. Reported content is reviewed by Spotts staff and removed if it violates our policies.

Your data, your rights

You can access, correct or request a copy of your personal data any time — email info@spottsapp.com. Requests are processed within 7 business days. See the Privacy Policy.

Your account

Deleting your account.

Your account is yours to close, any time. Deletion is immediate, permanent, and handled entirely by you — no phone call, no retention team.

In the app
Settings → Account → Delete Account
On the web
spottsapp.com/delete-account
Same result, without opening the app.
Just some of your data?
You can request deletion of specific data without closing your account — email info@spottsapp.com. Processed within 7 business days.
What happens when you delete
  • ✓

    Your name, email and phone number are permanently deleted.

  • ✓

    All active bookings are cancelled — refunds follow each venue's cancellation policy where applicable.

  • ✓

    Booking history, preferences and any earned rewards are gone with the account.

  • ✓

    Financial records are anonymised ("Deleted User") and kept 7 years, as Nigerian financial regulations require — then securely deleted.

  • ✓

    You get a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

This can't be undone. Once deleted, your account and its data can't be recovered.
